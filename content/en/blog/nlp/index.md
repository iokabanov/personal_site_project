## What is NLP?

Natural Language Processing, or NLP, is a field of artificial intelligence 
that focuses on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and generate 
human language. It sits at the intersection of linguistics, computer science, 
and machine learning, and is one of the most actively researched areas in AI today.

## Core Tasks in NLP

NLP covers a wide range of tasks, from simple to highly complex:

**Text classification** involves assigning a category to a piece of text, 
such as spam detection or sentiment analysis, where the goal is to determine 
whether a review is positive or negative.

**Named entity recognition (NER)** is the task of identifying and classifying 
named entities in text, such as people, organizations, and locations.

**Machine translation** is the automatic translation of text from one language 
to another. Tools like Google Translate and DeepL are built on NLP techniques.

**Text generation** involves producing coherent and contextually relevant text, 
which is the foundation of large language models like GPT and Claude.

**Question answering** enables systems to answer questions posed in natural 
language, either from a given document or from general knowledge.

## Key Techniques

Modern NLP is largely driven by deep learning. The transformer architecture, 
introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention is All You Need", revolutionized the 
field by enabling models to process entire sequences of text in parallel rather 
than word by word. This led to the development of large language models such as 
BERT, GPT, and T5, which achieved state of the art results across virtually 
every NLP benchmark.

Before transformers, NLP relied on recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and 
long short-term memory networks (LSTMs), which processed text sequentially 
and struggled with long-range dependencies.

## Applications of NLP

NLP powers many tools we use every day:

- Virtual assistants like Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant
- Search engines that understand the intent behind queries
- Grammar and writing tools like Grammarly
- Chatbots for customer support
- Automatic summarization of long documents
- Medical record analysis and clinical decision support

## Why NLP is Hard

Human language is full of ambiguity, context dependence, and nuance. The same 
sentence can mean completely different things depending on tone, context, or 
cultural background. Sarcasm, idioms, and metaphors are particularly challenging 
for machines to interpret correctly. Despite enormous progress, NLP systems 
still struggle with truly understanding language the way humans do.

## Getting Started with NLP

For anyone interested in exploring NLP, Python is the go-to language. The 
most popular libraries are NLTK for classical NLP tasks, spaCy for production 
ready pipelines, and the Hugging Face Transformers library for working with 
state of the art pretrained models. With just a few lines of code you can 
load a pretrained model and start classifying text, translating languages, 
or generating responses.
